How this model works

Payments arrive at the beginning of each month. The first payment earns interest for the full payment count; the last earns one month. The end age is the common valuation birthday. Effective annual returns convert to monthly returns as expm1(log1p(annual return) / 12). Income tax is deducted before investing; net contributions form the cost basis. Only positive investment growth is taxed at the endpoint, with no loss refund.

Constant payments and returns; no COLAs, new earnings, fees, volatility, quarterly taxes, deductions, loss carryforwards, or future law changes. Both strategies begin at or after full retirement age, so no earnings-test withholding is modeled. The $4,152 PIA is an illustrative input based on SSA's published 2026 maximum benefit at full retirement age, not a personalized entitlement or universal PIA maximum.
